🛡️ Job Safety Checklist

Before accepting a job or internship, check these warning signs:

  • ⚠️ The employer asks for registration or training fees.
  • ⚠️ The salary sounds unusually high for the work required.
  • ⚠️ The recruiter requests sensitive bank details too early.
  • ⚠️ The company has no verifiable official website.
  • ⚠️ The recruiter pressures you to accept immediately.

Verify the company independently before sharing personal information or accepting an offer.
